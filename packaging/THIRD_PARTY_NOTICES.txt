5ch Viewer - Third-party software notices / 第三者ソフトウェアのライセンス表記

This program uses the following third-party software.
本ソフトウェアは以下の第三者ソフトウェアを使用しています。

Windows build (5chviewer.exe) statically contains: wxWidgets 3.2 (with its bundled
libpng, zlib and regular-expression library), and the GCC / mingw-w64 runtime.
The installer is built with NSIS.
Linux builds (deb/rpm) link dynamically to the system's wxWidgets 3.2 and libcurl;
their licences are provided by the respective system packages.
